# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ICT staff and officers at San Jose Municipal Hall. They use the system during working hours at office workstations to manage, locate, and log movements of ICT devices. They are technically literate but the tool should not require expertise to navigate.

## Product Purpose

A web-based inventory and tracking management system for ICT devices owned by the municipal government. Lets staff record equipment, track device movements, scan QR codes, generate reports, and review audit logs. Success means any officer can find a device's status or history in under a minute.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, efficient, precise. Government-grade tool: no-frills, no decoration, no noise. Every element earns its place by helping staff complete a task faster.

## Anti-references

- Consumer apps with heavy personality (rounded bubbles, mascots, playful copy)
- SaaS dashboards optimized for demos (hero-metric grids, gradient accents, sparklines everywhere)
- Legacy government software (table-heavy, low contrast, no visual hierarchy)

## Design Principles

1. **Task first.** Every screen should make the primary action obvious within one second.
2. **Earned density.** Show enough data to be useful without overwhelming. Tables are appropriate here; decoration is not.
3. **Silent reliability.** The interface should feel like it never breaks. Errors are clear, states are handled, nothing feels unfinished.
4. **Contrast as hierarchy.** Weight and contrast do more work than color. Color signals state (active, error, success), not aesthetics.
5. **Both themes are first-class.** Light and dark modes get equal care. Neither is an afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA on both light and dark themes. Reduced-motion already handled in index.css. Color must not be the only signal for state (icons + labels supplement color).
